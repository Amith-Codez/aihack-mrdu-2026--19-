# Round 1 jury evaluation criteria (organizers' message, received 3 Oct 2026, about 21:00)

Pasted by the team lead into the planning chat. No weights were given.

The jury will primarily evaluate:
- Problem Understanding & Alignment
- Proposed Solution & Effectiveness
- Innovation & Differentiation
- Technical Approach & Implementation
- Prototype Functionality
- UX/UI & User Experience
- Scalability & Feasibility
- Technical Q&A
- Presentation & Demo
- Potential Impact & Use Case

"Please make sure your team is prepared to explain the problem, demonstrate the working prototype, justify your technical decisions, and answer questions from the jury."

Key focus for Round 1: Problem → Solution → Technology → Working Prototype → Impact
